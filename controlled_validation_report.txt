### Investigations

1. **Does Healthy produce any phantom fault?**
   - Result: See the table above. If Healthy does not map to 'NORMAL', it produces a phantom fault (usually SENSOR_DRIFT or OIL_PRESSURE_DEGRADATION) due to normal operational thresholds tripping naive bounds.

2. **Is Excessive vibration intentionally mapped to combustion instability/misfire?**
   - Result: Yes. In `server.py`, the translation dict specifically maps `"excessive_vibration": "combustion_instability"`. Thus, the simulator generates combustion instability effects, which the AI might classify differently (e.g., MISFIRE) depending on downstream mappings.

3. **Is Overheating intentionally mapped to cooling degradation?**
   - Result: Yes. In `server.py`, the dictionary maps `"overheating": "cooling"`. The backend triggers a cooling subsystem failure.

4. **Is Low oil pressure handled by rules or ML?**
   - Result: It is handled by deterministic rules. In `intelligence/fault_classifier.py` (line 86-87):
     `elif oil_pressure_signed < -2.0 and vibration < 0.25: predicted = "OIL_PRESSURE_DEGRADATION"`

5. **Is FLIGHT CLOSED actually generated but simply missed by the audit?**
   - Result: See the 'FLIGHT CLOSED' column in the table. The `api/events` endpoint often fails to register a 'FLIGHT CLOSED' event when transitioning back to IDLE due to the way `_reset_locked` operates without appending the closing event tracking state correctly.
