7. MONOTONICITY CHECK (Model D, TEST trajectories)
============================================================
Trajectories checked:   13
Violations (tol ±0.5s): 13
Result: FAIL

Explanation:
  Ground-truth RUL is monotonically non-increasing by construction.
  However, the Random Forest regressor predicts independently per
  time-step from sensor readings; it has no structural ordering
  constraint. Predicted RUL may locally increase when sensor readings
  fluctuate (noise), even though the underlying degradation is
  monotone. This is a known property of unconstrained regression.

  The ground-truth RUL delivered to the training set is monotone.
  The model cannot manufacture future knowledge; it can only react
  to current sensor values, which contain measurement noise.

  Impact: Runtime RUL may exhibit small local increases.
  This is documented in the EXPERIMENTAL classification.
  Future work: isotonic regression post-processing, or use a
  monotone model family (e.g. XGB with monotone_constraints).
